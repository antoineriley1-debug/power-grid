How to Play

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the 8×8 grid. Pieces don't rotate and nothing falls.
  • Fill a whole row or column to clear it and restore power.
  • Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing on back-to-back moves, for a combo multiplier.
  • Generator tiles (lightning bolt): when a cleared line passes through one, it fires and also clears its crossing line. Generators can set off other generators.
  • Combos fill the Surge meter. When it's full, your next piece is a blast piece: it also clears the 3×3 area around its center.
  • Your first big combo earns 1 Undo for the game.
  • Score milestones light up the city skyline building by building. Light all 10 to power the district and move on to the next one.
  • The game ends when none of your tray pieces fits.
  • Settings: Block size scales the grid and tray pieces; High-contrast adds outlines and a shape mark per color; Show next tray previews the next deal (the fit guarantee may still swap one piece in).

How it works (fair play)

Pieces come from a fixed list with fixed weights, so the odds never change based on your score or how you're doing. There's no rigging and no ads.

With Fit guarantee on, each new tray is checked: if none of the 3 pieces fits but some piece could, one random slot is swapped for a piece that fits.

Daily Grid uses a seed built from today's date, so everyone gets the same piece sequence. A fit-guarantee swap uses a separate random stream, so the main sequence stays the same.
